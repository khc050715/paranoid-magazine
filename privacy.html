Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Paranoid Magazine uses a small private tool to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel. The tool is used only by the channel owner.

What we access

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload to upload videos. It does not read, collect, store or share any other Google user data.

What we store

An authorisation token is kept on the owner's own computer so uploads can run. It is never shared with anyone.

Third parties

We do not sell or share data. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos are published under YouTube's Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Send a message to @paranoid_magazine on Instagram.
