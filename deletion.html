Data Deletion

Paranoid Magazine's tool only publishes our own videos to our own Facebook Page, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It does not collect or store data about other users.

If you want any data related to you removed, send a message to @paranoid_magazine on Instagram. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

You can also remove the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites.
